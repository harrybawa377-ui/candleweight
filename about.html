About Candle weight

Candle weight is a market-data and analytics platform built to help users explore what happens inside every candle. A price chart shows the movement of the market, but each candle contains much more information. Candle weight brings together detailed market data such as OHLC prices, candle range, body size, upper and lower wicks, percentage change, volume, traded quantity, market activity and other calculated statistics.

Go Deeper Into Every Candle

Our purpose is to make 1-minute market data easier to study and compare. Instead of looking only at the visual shape of a candle, users can examine the numerical data behind it and understand how price, volume and activity changed during each minute.

Data, Calculations and Statistics

Candle weight focuses on presenting market data in a clear and structured format. Historical comparisons, minute-by-minute statistics and calculated measurements can help users study how current market activity compares with previous periods.

Built for Data Analysis

Candle weight does not provide trading recommendations, predictions or buy and sell signals. The platform is designed to provide data and analytical information so users can perform their own research and make their own decisions.

Explore the Data Behind Every Candle

Every candle is a collection of measurable data points. Candle weight brings those details together in one place, making it easier to explore, compare and analyse market activity—one minute at a time.

🎯 Our Mission

We aim to equip independent financial analysts, algorithmic quantitative traders, and institutional researchers with instantaneous order depth visibility and effortless streaming tools.
