Understanding Historical Average Weight Calculations

The historical log table displays real-time order flow progression row by row. The 3rd column (Calculated Weight) represents the raw, instantaneous Candle Weight score generated for a specific time interval, while the 2nd column (Average Weight) displays the running average of those weight values up to that specific timestamp. For example, at Row 10, the Average Weight column calculates the mean score of the Calculated Weight values across the first 10 rows (Rows 1 through 10). This running average smooths out temporary market noise and brief volatility spikes recorded in the 4th column (Time), helping traders easily identify sustained institutional accumulation or distribution trends over time.

The CandleWeight Historical Archive provides traders and quantitative analysts with access to past order flow delta, market depth recordings, and candle weight calculations.
